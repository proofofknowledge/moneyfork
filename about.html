About MoneyFork

Every major financial decision is a fork in the road — rent or buy, pay debt or invest, Roth or Traditional. These choices shape your financial future, and most people make them based on gut feeling, outdated rules of thumb, or advice that doesn't account for their specific situation.

MoneyFork exists to change that.

We build free, interactive financial calculators that show you both paths with real math — not opinions. Every tool uses actual financial formulas, real tax brackets, and proper compounding so you can see exactly what each choice means for your money.

Our Tools

MoneyFork currently offers six decision tools covering the most common financial crossroads: Rent vs Buy, Debt vs Invest, Roth vs Traditional, House Affordability, Compound Interest, and Emergency Fund. We're building more — each one designed to answer a specific question with real numbers.

Not Financial Advice

MoneyFork is an educational tool. We're not financial advisors, and our calculators don't account for every variable in your situation. They're designed to give you a solid starting point and help you think through decisions more clearly. For major financial decisions, we always recommend consulting with a qualified financial professional.
