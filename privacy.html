Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

The short version: We don't collect your financial data. All calculator inputs stay in your browser. We use basic analytics to understand how people use the site so we can make it better.

What We Don't Collect

MoneyFork calculators run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your financial inputs (income, debts, savings, etc.) are never sent to our servers or any third party. When you close the page, your data is gone. We don't store it, we don't sell it, we don't share it — because we never had it.

What We Do Collect

We use Google Analytics to understand basic site usage — things like which pages are visited, how long people stay, and what devices they use. This helps us know which calculators are most useful and where to improve. Google Analytics collects anonymized data such as page views, referring URLs, browser type, and general geographic region. It does not have access to any financial data you enter into our calculators.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish unique visitors. These are standard analytics cookies and do not contain personal financial information. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Third-Party Links

MoneyFork may contain links to third-party websites or services (such as financial products or educational resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

Advertising

We may display advertisements through third-party ad networks such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of MoneyFork after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our contact page.
